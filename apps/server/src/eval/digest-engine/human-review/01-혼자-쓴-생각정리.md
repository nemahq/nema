## 1. [decision] Exclude B2B collaboration tool projects

- **situation**: Founding a business while bound by a 1-year non-compete clause from a previous job in the B2B collaboration tool sector.
- **choice**: Exclude all candidates related to B2B collaboration tools
- **reason**: Legally bound by a non-compete agreement.
- **tradeoff**: 
  - Limits potential business ideas in a familiar domain

## 2. [decision] Strategy for early product development

- **situation**: Planning the development path for the chosen business candidate.
- **choice**: Start with a thin, self-service model rather than a full-service approach
- **reason**: Learning from a case study showing that lowering adoption barriers is more critical for initial growth than adding more features.
- **tradeoff**: 
  - Limits initial feature depth
- **alternatives**: 
  - Full-service model

## 3. [decision] Focus on Onboarding Automation for initial interviews

- **situation**: Need to validate potential startup ideas.
- **choice**: Proceed with 5 interviews focused on the Onboarding Automation candidate
- **reason**: It ranks highest in problem intensity and clear accountability, and there is a personal interest that may indicate a strong signal.
- **tradeoff**: 
  - Postpones deep investigation into other candidates
- **alternatives**: 
  - Interview candidates for Document Version Control

## 4. [pending] Validation of accountability for Document Version Control

- **question**: Who is responsible for the problem of internal document version control?
- **background**: The candidate has high non-substitutability but ambiguous accountability ('everyone is slightly responsible').
- **branches**: 
  - Determine if there is a specific owner worth targeting
  - Discard if no clear owner is found
- **resolutionCondition**: Confirm through future interviews whether a specific individual takes responsibility for this problem.

## 5. [learning] Importance of adoption barriers

- **finding**: For B2B tools, lowering the barrier to adoption is more effective for initial growth than adding more features.
- **evidence**: Reference to a case study of a B2B company that saw growth only after pivoting from full-service to self-service for SMEs.

## 6. [idea] Personal interest as a signal for business selection

- **concept**: The subjective 'pull' or interest towards a specific problem may be a valid signal for business viability, even if not based on personal pain.
- **background**: Noticed that while some ideas seem profitable, they lack personal resonance, whereas Onboarding Automation creates a natural attraction.

## 7. [assumption] Assessment criteria for startup candidates

- **assumption**: A valid startup problem must meet criteria of high intensity, clear accountability, high frequency, and high non-substitutability.
- **evidence**: Current filter set used to evaluate potential ideas.
- **impact**: If these criteria are flawed, time may be wasted on non-viable problems or good opportunities may be ignored.
- **verificationCondition**: Real-world validation through interviews and market response.
