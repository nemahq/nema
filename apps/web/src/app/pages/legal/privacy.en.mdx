# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Kwangyeol Lim (the "Operator") collects, uses, stores, and disposes of personal information for the Nema service (the "Service"), and explains users' rights and how to exercise them. This policy has been prepared in accordance with the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and related laws.


## Article 1 (Items and Methods of Personal Information Collection)

### 1. Items Collected

| Category     | Items Collected                                          | Purpose                                               |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Required     | Email address, name, profile photo (via Google OAuth)    | User identification, login, service provision          |
| Service use  | Content directly entered by users (memos, notes, etc.)   | Core feature provision (AI-based content organization and search) |
| Auto-collect | IP address, browser type, access time, usage logs        | Service stability, error response, statistical analysis |

### 2. Methods of Collection

- Automatic collection during Google OAuth 2.0 social login
- Direct input by users during service use
- Automatic generation and collection during service use


## Article 2 (Purpose of Collection and Use)

The Operator uses collected personal information for the following purposes:

1. **User management**: User identification, identity verification, registration and withdrawal processing
2. **Service provision**: AI-based content organization and search, personalized service operation
3. **Service improvement**: Usage statistics analysis, service quality enhancement, new feature development
4. **Stable operation**: System error detection and response, fraud prevention, security maintenance
5. **Notices and guidance**: Delivery of service changes and announcements, inquiry response


## Article 3 (Retention and Use Period)

1. Users' personal information is retained and used during the service use period. Upon account deletion, it is retained in a recoverable state for 30 days before being destroyed.
2. However, if retention is required by applicable laws, the information is stored for the period specified by such laws.

| Legal Basis                              | Retained Items                              | Period   |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Act on Consumer Protection in E-Commerce | Records of contracts or subscription withdrawal | 5 years  |
| Act on Consumer Protection in E-Commerce | Records of consumer complaints or disputes  | 3 years  |
| Protection of Communications Secrets Act | Access log records                           | 3 months |


## Article 4 (Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)

The Operator does not, in principle, provide users' personal information to third parties. However, exceptions are made in the following cases:

1. When the user has given prior consent
2. When required by law or upon lawful request by investigative authorities

## Article 7 (Procedures and Methods of Personal Information Destruction)

1. **Destruction procedure**: Personal information is destroyed without delay when the purpose of use is achieved or the retention period has expired. If retention is required by law, the information is moved to a separate database or stored in a distinguished location, then destroyed upon expiration.
2. **Destruction methods**
   - Electronic files: Permanently deleted using methods that prevent recovery
   - Other records: Shredded or incinerated


## Article 8 (Rights and Obligations of Users and Legal Representatives)

1. Users (or legal representatives) may exercise the following rights regarding their personal information at any time:
   - Request to view personal information
   - Request to correct or delete personal information
   - Request to suspend processing of personal information
   - Account deletion (withdrawal of consent)
2. These rights may be exercised through account settings within the Service or via email (mkitigy@gmail.com). The Operator will take necessary action without delay. Upon account deletion, personal information is retained for 30 days for account recovery purposes.
3. If a user requests correction or deletion of personal information, the Operator will not use or provide such information until the correction or deletion is completed.
4. Personal information of children under 14 is not collected, and children under 14 cannot register for the Service.


## Article 9 (Measures to Ensure Safety of Personal Information)

The Operator takes the following safety measures in accordance with Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act:

1. **Administrative measures**: Minimizing personnel handling personal information, establishing and implementing internal management plans
2. **Technical measures**: Encryption of personal information (TLS for data in transit, encrypted storage for sensitive data), security software operation, access control management
3. **Physical measures**: Utilizing physical security systems of cloud service providers


## Article 10 (Installation, Operation, and Rejection of Automatic Collection Devices)

1. The Operator may use cookies and other automatic collection devices for service operation.
2. **Purpose of cookies**: Maintaining login sessions, providing service convenience, service improvement through usage pattern analysis
3. **How to reject cookies**: Users can allow or reject cookies through web browser settings.
   - Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
   - Safari: Preferences > Privacy
   - Rejecting cookies may restrict certain services such as login.
